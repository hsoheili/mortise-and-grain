Wood moves when it changes climate. A piece shipped from Kingston in February into a dry apartment will shrink slightly across its width over the first month. That is normal and designed for; nothing will crack.

Returns

Stock pieces can come back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, less the return shipping. They need to be in the condition they left in. Send a photograph first and we will arrange the collection.

Small goods, the same 30 days, but a cutting board that has been used is not returnable — once it has been washed and oiled it is yours.

Why commissions are different

A commission is cut to your dimensions in a species you chose. We cannot sell a 94-inch walnut table with your mother's initials under the apron to anybody else. So commissioned work is not returnable, and the deposit is not refundable once we have started milling.

What we do instead is show you the work before it is finished. You get photographs at the rough-milled stage, at dry-fit, and before finishing, and you can change your mind at each of those points while changing it is still cheap. The full sequence is set out on the Custom Work page.
